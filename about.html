Our Background

Eighteen years of mechanical rooms, risers and residential repipes.

The company was founded by two journeymen who spent their twenties maintaining boiler rooms and grease interceptors for restaurant groups across Wake County. When those same operators asked them to fix the plumbing at home, the residential division was born — and it inherited the commercial playbook.

Today Apex runs 14 service trucks, a fabrication bench, three staged yards and a central dispatch office staffed by people who have actually worked in the field. We are large enough to answer at 2 AM and small enough that the owner still reviews escalated jobs on Fridays.

  • 2008 Founded in Raleigh

    Two journeyman plumbers incorporate Apex Mechanical with a single van and a commercial kitchen contract.

  • 2014 Residential division launched

    Flat-rate pricing introduced company-wide after a customer survey ranked “hourly billing anxiety” as the number one complaint about the trade.

  • 2019 24/7 dispatch desk established

    In-house night dispatch replaces a third-party answering service. Emergency response times drop from 2.4 hours to under 45 minutes.

  • 2024 Third staging yard opens

    West Durham yard comes online, cutting average arrival time in Orange and Durham counties by 19 minutes.
