Pricing Questions

Why a flat rate instead of hourly?

Because the hourly model rewards slow work. We price the job, not the clock — and our technicians are not compensated on parts sold.

Ask a billing question
Is the starting price the final price?

Not always — but the only number that matters is the written quote you approve on site. Starting prices assume standard access and code-compliant existing conditions. If we find something different, you get a revised written quote and a choice, not a bigger invoice after the fact.

Why do some companies charge less for a water heater?

Usually because the quote excludes permit, expansion control, new shut-off, haul-away or code corrections — line items that appear later. Our number includes them so the comparison is honest.

Do you charge a trip fee if I decline the quote?

Only the $89 diagnostic fee. There is no additional trip charge, no dispatch fee and no pressure. If you decline, you owe $89 and we leave the written quote with you for reference.

Can you match a competitor’s written quote?

When the scope matches, we will review it. Send the written quote to dispatch@apexmechanical.example and a manager will respond within one business day with a like-for-like comparison.

How does financing affect the price?

The project price does not change. Financing is provided by a third-party lender, and the terms you receive depend on credit approval. We disclose the lender’s estimate before you apply, and there is no prepayment penalty.
