Straight Answers

Questions we get before every first visit.

If your question is not here, call the dispatch desk. A human answers, and they can see today’s schedule.

Contact dispatch
Is the $89 diagnostic fee really waived?

Yes. The fee covers the on-site inspection, camera work and written quote. If you approve any repair during that visit, the $89 is credited in full. If you decline, you owe the $89 and nothing else.

How fast can you actually get here?

For active emergencies inside our core metro ZIPs, the current average is 38 minutes and our contractual ceiling is 90 minutes. Scheduled appointments are confirmed in two-hour windows the day before.

Are your technicians employees or subcontractors?

Employees. Every field technician is W-2, background-checked, drug-screened and enrolled in continuing education. We do not broker residential work to third-party crews.

Do you pull permits?

On every job that requires one — water heater replacements, repipes, main line work and gas line modifications. The permit fee is included in your quote, and the inspection is scheduled by our office, not left to you.

What if the repair fails after you leave?

Call the same number. Workmanship is covered for 10 years on qualifying installations and 1 year on standard repairs, with a 90-day callback guarantee on drain clearing. We return at no charge if our work is at fault.
