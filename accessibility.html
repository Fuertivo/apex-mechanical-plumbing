Accessibility Statement

A plumbing emergency is stressful enough without a website that will not work with your screen reader or keyboard. Here is what we have done, and how to tell us when we have missed something.

Last updated: October 1, 2026Target: WCAG 2.1 Level AA

1. Our commitment

Apex Mechanical & Plumbing Solutions is committed to making this website usable by the widest possible audience, regardless of ability or technology. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA and to maintain that conformance as the site changes.

2. Measures we have taken

  • Semantic HTML landmarks, headings and lists throughout the site
  • Keyboard-operable navigation with a visible skip-to-content link
  • Visible focus indicators on all interactive elements
  • Color combinations tested for contrast, including amber-on-navy and navy-on-white
  • Text descriptions for decorative and informational graphics where they convey meaning
  • Form labels tied to inputs, with descriptive error handling
  • Support for the operating system’s reduced-motion preference, including disabling the pulsing emergency banner
  • Responsive layouts that reflow to 320 CSS pixels without loss of content
  • Accessible alternatives to web scheduling: our dispatch line is answered by a person 24/7

3. Conformance status

This site is designed to target WCAG 2.1 AA. The demonstration build has been reviewed manually for semantic structure, keyboard operability, focus order, contrast and reduced-motion behavior. It has not yet undergone a full third-party accessibility audit, and no formal certification is claimed.

4. Known limitations

  • Embedded scheduling module. The calendar placeholder on the scheduling page will eventually host a third-party scheduling embed. Its accessibility depends on that provider, and we will require a documented accessibility conformance statement before integration.
  • Static map placeholder. The map graphic on the contact page is currently an illustration rather than an interactive map. Address details are provided in text immediately adjacent to it.
  • PDF documents. Invoices, permits and warranty documents may be provided as PDFs. If you need an alternate format, call our office and we will provide one at no cost.

5. Feedback and assistance

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, or need information from this site in another format, contact us:

  • Phone: (555) 014-7700 — 24 hours a day
  • Email: dispatch@apexmechanical.example
  • Mail: 1420 Foundry Row, Suite 300, Raleigh, NC 27601

We aim to acknowledge accessibility feedback within two business days and to provide a substantive response within ten business days. If you need help scheduling service because a web form is not usable for you, call the dispatch line and we will complete the request by phone.

6. Ongoing effort

Accessibility is maintained through design review, manual testing and periodic re-audit. When we add features — including the production calendar module — accessibility requirements are part of the acceptance criteria, not a retrofit.
