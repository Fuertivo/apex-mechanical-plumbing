Terms of Service

The terms that apply when you request, schedule or receive service from Apex Mechanical & Plumbing Solutions.

Last updated: October 1, 2026Effective for services booked on or after this date

Demonstration notice. This is sample content for a fictional company and is not legal advice. Have final terms reviewed by qualified counsel in your jurisdiction before publication.

1. Scope of services

Apex Mechanical & Plumbing Solutions provides plumbing, mechanical and drain services in North Carolina under applicable state and local licenses. Services are performed by employees or, where disclosed in advance, qualified subcontractors. We reserve the right to decline work that is unsafe, outside our licensing scope, or subject to conditions we cannot reasonably control.

2. Estimates, quotes and pricing

Starting rates published on this website are entry points for standard residential access and normal working hours. They are not offers for a specific job. A binding price is established only by a written quote presented after on-site assessment and approved by you.

Quotes remain valid for 30 days unless otherwise stated. If concealed conditions are discovered — including but not limited to code violations, asbestos, structural damage, or inaccessible piping — work stops and a revised written quote is presented for approval before proceeding.

3. Scheduling and arrival windows

Appointment requests submitted through this website are requests, not confirmed bookings. An appointment is confirmed only when a dispatcher contacts you and you accept the offered arrival window. Arrival windows are estimates; traffic, weather, prior emergencies and site access can affect timing.

If we cannot meet a confirmed window, we will contact you with a revised time. If the revised time does not work, you may reschedule at no charge.

4. Cancellation and access

You may reschedule or cancel without charge up to two hours before the confirmed arrival window. Cancellations inside two hours, or an inability to access the service location when the technician arrives, may incur a $59 missed-access fee. The fee is disclosed before it is charged.

You agree to provide safe, reasonable access to the work area, including crawlspaces, mechanical closets and shut-off valves, and to secure pets and remove valuables from the work area. Additional charges may apply if access requires return visits or specialized equipment not included in the original quote.

5. Emergency service

Emergency dispatch is available 24 hours a day. After-hours calls may carry a $149 dispatch fee, disclosed before a technician is assigned. Emergency work is quoted and approved before repair begins whenever conditions permit; where immediate action is required to prevent injury or further property damage, you authorize the minimum necessary protective work at published emergency rates.

6. Payment terms

Residential payment is due upon completion unless a written financing arrangement is in place. We accept major credit cards, ACH transfer and check. Commercial accounts may apply for Net-30 terms subject to credit approval. Returned payments may incur a fee permitted by law.

Invoices more than 30 days past due may accrue interest at the maximum rate permitted by North Carolina law. You agree to pay reasonable collection costs, including attorney fees, where permitted.

7. Warranty

Apex provides a workmanship warranty on labor as stated on your invoice: 10 years on qualifying installations, one year on standard repairs, and a 90-day callback guarantee on drain clearing. Manufacturer warranties pass through to you and are provided in writing where available.

The workmanship warranty does not cover damage caused by freezing, power loss, water quality, misuse, third-party work, acts of God, or pre-existing conditions documented at the time of service. Warranty service requires access to the work area and may be voided if the installation is altered by others.

8. Property condition and pre-existing damage

Plumbing repairs sometimes require opening walls, ceilings, floors or landscaping. Our quotes describe anticipated access and restoration. Where restoration is excluded, that exclusion is stated in writing. We are not responsible for pre-existing structural, cosmetic or moisture damage, or for damage caused by concealed conditions that could not reasonably have been discovered.

9. Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, Apex Mechanical & Plumbing Solutions’ total liability arising from any service is limited to the amount paid for that service. We are not liable for indirect, incidental, special or consequential damages, including lost profits, lost use of property, or water damage resulting from a pre-existing failure we did not cause.

Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited by law, including liability for gross negligence or willful misconduct.

10. Permits, inspections and code compliance

Where a permit is required, we file it and coordinate the inspection as part of the quoted price. Work is performed to the code in effect at the time of permitting. If a municipality requires additional work, you will receive a written change order before it is performed.

11. Disputes and governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of North Carolina. Before filing a legal claim, you agree to contact us and allow 30 days for good-faith resolution. Any unresolved dispute shall be brought in the state or federal courts located in Wake County, North Carolina, unless applicable law requires otherwise.

12. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The version in effect when you book a service applies to that service. Continued use of this website after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms for future requests.
