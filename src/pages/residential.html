Replace the plumbing, not the drywall.

When To Call

Signs that are cheaper to fix now.

Not every symptom is an emergency. These are the ones that get materially more expensive if you wait a season.

The meter spins with everything off

A supply-side leak is running somewhere. Shut the main off and call. Waiting can undermine a slab or saturate a crawlspace.

Pressure drops when a second tap runs

Classic internal corrosion or an undersized line. A pressure test will tell you whether it is a repair or a repipe year.

A drain gurgles or backs up repeatedly

Repeat backups usually mean a partial blockage or a belly in the line. Camera it once instead of augering it four times.

Hot water runs out faster than it used to

Sediment, a failing element or a tank near the end of its life. A $90 flush is cheaper than a flooded garage.

A wet patch in the yard that will not dry

Usually the main service line or a sewer lateral. Locating it early keeps the repair from taking out a driveway.

You are planning a renovation

Bring us in before the walls close. Moving a stack or upsizing a line after tile is a much more expensive conversation.

Residential Questions

What homeowners ask us most.

Still unsure? Call dispatch at (555) 014-7700. No charge for a straight answer.

Do you charge extra for weekends or nights?

Standard hours are Monday through Sunday, 7 AM to 7 PM, at the published rates. Emergency calls outside those hours carry a disclosed after-hours surcharge, quoted on the phone before a technician is dispatched.

Can I supply my own water heater or fixtures?

Yes, with a caveat. We install customer-supplied fixtures and heaters, but the workmanship warranty applies only to our labor — manufacturer defects and sizing errors become your responsibility. We will tell you before installing anything out of spec.

Will you need to shut off the water all day?

Not usually. Most repairs isolate a single branch. Repipes may require an overnight shutdown; we leave a temporary hose bib live and tell you the night before.

How do I know the technician is legitimate?

You receive their name, photo and license number by text before arrival. Every truck is marked, every technician carries a photo ID, and our dispatch desk can verify employment while they are at your door.

Do you offer any payment plans?

Yes. Approved projects over $2,500 can be financed through our third-party lending partner with terms from 12 to 60 months. Applications are soft-pull and take about three minutes on the technician’s tablet.
