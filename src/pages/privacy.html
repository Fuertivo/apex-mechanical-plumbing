Privacy Policy

How Apex Mechanical & Plumbing Solutions collects, uses and protects information submitted through appointment requests, service calls and this website.

Last updated: October 1, 2026Applies to: apexmechanical.example

Demonstration notice. This is a sample policy for a fictional company. It is not legal advice. Replace it with a policy reviewed by qualified counsel before publishing a real website.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly when you request an appointment, call dispatch, submit a commercial proposal request, apply for a job or correspond with our office. This includes your name, service address, phone number, email address, property details, service history, payment information and any notes you provide about the issue.

We also collect limited technical information automatically when you use this website, such as browser type, device type, referring page, and pages viewed. This information is aggregated and is not used to identify you individually.

2. How we use information

  • To schedule, dispatch and complete service calls
  • To prepare quotes, invoices and warranty documentation
  • To contact you about an appointment, estimate or follow-up
  • To maintain service history for warranty and maintenance purposes
  • To process payments and communicate with lenders if you apply for financing
  • To comply with licensing, permitting and tax obligations
  • To improve routing, response times and customer service

We do not sell customer information, and we do not share it with third-party advertisers.

3. When we share information

We share information only with parties necessary to complete the work or operate the business, including:

  • Technicians and dispatch staff assigned to your job
  • Payment processors and, if applicable, financing partners
  • Suppliers and subcontractors required for a specific scope, with your knowledge
  • Municipal permitting and inspection authorities where required by law
  • Professional advisers, insurers and auditors
  • Government authorities when compelled by lawful request

These parties receive only the information necessary for their function and are obligated to protect it.

4. Calls, texts and marketing

By submitting an appointment request, you consent to be contacted by phone, text message or email about that request. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of marketing messages at any time by replying STOP to a text or by contacting our office. Opting out of marketing does not prevent us from contacting you about an active service call, invoice or warranty matter.

We do not use automatic telephone dialing systems or prerecorded messages for marketing without prior express written consent.

5. Cookies and analytics

This website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences and measure aggregate traffic. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies does not prevent you from requesting service by phone.

If analytics or advertising tools are added in the future, this section will be updated and, where required, a consent mechanism will be presented before non-essential cookies are set.

6. Retention and security

Service records are retained for at least the duration of the applicable warranty plus any period required by law. We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration and loss. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; if a breach affecting your information occurs, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

7. Your choices and rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to legal and contractual retention requirements. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will verify your identity before acting on a request and respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

North Carolina residents and residents of other states with applicable privacy statutes may have additional rights, including the right to know what categories of information are collected and the right to opt out of certain uses. We do not sell personal information.

8. Children’s privacy

Our services are directed to adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact our office and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be posted on this page, and where required, communicated directly.
