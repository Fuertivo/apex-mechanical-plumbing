The Apprentice Track

Paid to learn. Not paid to fetch.

A two-year structured program with documented hours toward your journeyman license, a mentor assigned for the full term, and a raise at each milestone.

  1. 01

    Months 1–6 — Ride and learn

    Assigned to a senior technician, you learn diagnostics, code basics and job-site etiquette. Classroom hours are paid, and textbooks are covered.

  2. 02

    Months 7–18 — Lead small work

    You run simple service calls under remote supervision, take your backflow certification, and start logging the hours your license requires.

  3. 03

    Months 19–24 — License and launch

    Exam prep is paid, fees are covered, and you transition to a service plumber role with your own truck and route territory.

Hiring Process

Four steps, two weeks.

No ten-round interview theater. A phone screen, a working conversation, a ride-along and a decision.

  • Step 1Phone screen

    Fifteen minutes covering license status, experience and schedule.

  • Step 2Technical conversation

    A service manager walks through two real scenarios. No trick questions.

  • Step 3Paid ride-along

    A half-day with a crew, paid at your proposed rate, so you can judge us too.

  • Step 4Offer

    Written offer with pay, shift, benefits and the next review date in writing.
