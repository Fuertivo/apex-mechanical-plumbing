Before Your Visit

Three things that make the visit faster.

Prep 01

Locate your main shut-off

Know where the whole-house valve is before we arrive. If you cannot find it, our dispatcher can walk you through the usual locations for your builder’s era of home.

Prep 02

Clear the mechanical space

Twelve inches of clearance around the water heater, sump pit and main line lets the technician work safely and shortens the job — occasionally shortening the invoice.

Prep 03

Write down the symptoms

When the noise starts, when the pressure drops, which fixtures are affected. A clear timeline saves diagnostic time and gets you a sharper quote.
