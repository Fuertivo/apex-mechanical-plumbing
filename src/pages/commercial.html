Commercial Questions

Procurement, access and invoicing.

Do you carry the insurance limits our property requires?

We carry $2,000,000 general liability and $1,000,000 workers’ compensation, and we can add your entity as an additional insured on request. Certificates are issued within one business day, naming your management company and ownership entity as required.

Can you work outside our operating hours?

Yes. Restaurant and retail accounts are typically serviced between 10 PM and 6 AM. Medical and office accounts are scheduled before opening or after close. Night work is priced at the same flat rate as day work for contract accounts.

How are invoices structured?

One invoice per site visit, with asset tags, work performed, parts and labor separated, and the purchase order reference printed on the document. Consolidated monthly billing and Net-30 terms are available after credit approval.

Do you handle permits and inspections?

Yes. We file permits, coordinate the inspection and keep the signed-off documentation in your account file. You receive a copy with the closing invoice, and we track renewal dates for backflow assemblies.

What happens if a job exceeds the approved quote?

Work stops. Your account manager calls the listed site contact with a written change order and photos. Nothing proceeds without an approval reference recorded in the job file — the same standard we hold on residential work.
